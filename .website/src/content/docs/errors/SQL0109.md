---
title: "alert evidence does not meet measurement requirements"
slug: errors/SQL0109
kind: "event"
level: "warn"
consequence: "recorded alert unchanged"
---

A scheduled alert check read a measurement that cannot establish the alert
condition. The measurement's identity, kind, unit, value, metadata, or time
does not satisfy the evaluator's requirements. The warning names the alert
and owner, with the specific requirement in `detail`.

For example, stream 42, `signup.welcome-email`, has a newest fresh partition
measurement of `-1`. Its `partition_count` check warns with
`stream_id=42` and `detail="partition count must be a non-negative integer within int64 range"`.
The check leaves any recorded alert unchanged and increments the failed-stream
count. It cannot establish whether the stream has too many partitions.

Read the alert's [Snapshot](/reference/alerts/) to inspect `State`,
`EvidenceInvalid`, `Reason`, and the evidence times. Check the named
measurement and its required metadata, correct the source of the rejected
data, and restore successful collection. A fresh valid measurement lets the
next check evaluate the condition again; existing pending rules still apply.

Missing or stale measurements and absent manager coverage log at DEBUG.
Sustained missing collection uses the existing collector-progress alert.
Read and decode errors keep their returned-error behavior. Registration
keeps all insufficient-evidence results at DEBUG.

The logger can suppress repeated warnings across different owners. A warning
is not a complete list of affected streams; use the failed-stream counts
and individual snapshots to find the affected checks. Nothing is produced
to `__system.alerts` for this warning.
